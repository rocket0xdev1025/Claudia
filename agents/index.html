The MCP server

https://claudia.gallery/mcp speaks the Model Context Protocol over Streamable HTTP. It is public and read-only: no account, no key, no state.

Connect

Claude Code:

claude mcp add --transport http claudia https://claudia.gallery/mcp

Claude (web and desktop): Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, and paste https://claudia.gallery/mcp.

Cursor, Windsurf and other clients that take a JSON config:

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "claudia": {
      "url": "https://claudia.gallery/mcp"
    }
  }
}

Codex CLI (~/.codex/config.toml):

[mcp_servers.claudia]
url = "https://claudia.gallery/mcp"

Clients that only speak stdio can bridge with npx mcp-remote https://claudia.gallery/mcp.

Tools

claudia_canon

Who Claudia is, in one call: the constants that never change (hair, the single clay streak, the star clip, the headset mic, face, age), the identity text to paste into every image prompt (a full form for close and medium shots, a short form for full length and wider), every look's wardrobe text across the three films, her voice and manner, the never-list, and the norms for releasing songs with her (your own artist name, her credit in the description, an AI label only if AI made it). Call this first before making anything with her.

Arguments: include_images.

list_films

The three music videos Claudia stars in (ESCAPE VELOCITY, CLODYSSEY, BLISS): loglines, the world of each, measured numbers (length, shots, Midjourney prompts and images, Seedance clips), image counts in this gallery, links to the film pages and streams, and the list of each film's making-of documents.

Arguments: none.

browse_images

Page through the gallery's Midjourney images. Filter by film, by set (in_film: plates used in the finished film; her: any image with Claudia; all), by batch, look or framing. Returns compact entries: id, film, batch, job, variant, flags, size, image URLs and the start of the prompt. Use get_image for the full prompt.

Arguments: film, set, batch, look, framing, offset, limit.

search_images

Full-text search over every prompt in the gallery (1,562 Midjourney images), plus ids, batches and looks. All words must match (falls back to any word). Plates used in the film and the director's picks rank first on ties. Example queries: 'chroma-key green', 'porcelain mask', 'robotaxi night', 'sky gown hill'.

Arguments: query, film, set, limit.

get_image

One Midjourney image by id (e.g. 'sp-02-open.siren-mcu.2'): the exact prompt and parameters it ran with, whether it is a plate in the finished film, the director's verdict if any, its sibling variants, the batch note and URLs at three sizes. Set include_image to receive the picture itself so you can look at her.

Arguments: id, include_image, size.

get_guide

An article of the Claudia wiki (claudia.gallery/wiki) as Markdown, distilled from the three productions. start: making a film of your own (the six checkpoints where a person decides, the accounts, the spending rules, the first conversation, what to build first). consistency: keeping Claudia herself across thousands of images and clips. song: Suno, lyrics that land, scansion and assonance, her voice. storyboard: shots, one explaining graphic per line, the hook, cut rhythm, the audit, the director's review. midjourney: writing and picking plates. seedance: moving shots, structured prompts, measured lip sync. motion: motion design and the edit in code. pipeline: the whole production and its numbers. toolkit: the spec of every tool behind the films (the lyric scorer, alignment and the ear check, the beat map, hits, the board audit, the clean vocal window, the spectrogram sync check, covers, the edit list, the renderer, the lyric gate, the flash and history checks, the reviewer, encoding), with constants and acceptance checks, so they can be rebuilt. formats: the data files the tools share. failures: what went wrong and the rule each failure left. Long guides can be read one section at a time with section.

Arguments: topic, section.

plan_film

Turn a scale, a song length and the accounts someone has into a stage-by-stage plan for a Claudia music video, the way the films were made: the recommended scale (quick, studio or full) and why, estimates (shots, clip seconds, Seedance cost at 2x, Midjourney jobs), what is missing, the first-conversation questions with defaults, the stages with the six checkpoints where the director decides, and the tools to build in order with links to their specs.

Arguments: scale, song_seconds, has, budget_usd.

get_record

A film's making-of document as Markdown: readme (the story, the stack, every step, the numbers), prompts-midjourney, prompts-seedance, prompts-suno, and for bliss also xp-desktop-and-sound (how the XP desktop engine and its sound layer were built). They are long (up to about 150 KB): read in pages with offset and max_chars; the reply says where the next page starts.

Arguments: film, doc, offset, max_chars.

build_prompt

Compose a Midjourney v8.2 prompt for Claudia from the canon, the way the films did: framing first, her identity text (the full form for close and medium shots, the short form for full length and wider), a look's wardrobe (or your own), your scene, then one of the films' style families, ending 'no text, no letters, no logos'. Returns the prompt, the parameters and a checklist for the result.

Arguments: scene, framing, look, wardrobe, style, light, aspect.

get_skill

How to install the Claudia agent skill (the canon, every guide from Start here to the toolkit spec, the films' prompt records and a stdlib-only fetch script, as a zip), and the site's other machine-readable endpoints (llms.txt, the JSON API, Markdown versions of every page).

Arguments: none.

Resources and prompts

Resources: claudia://canon, claudia://wiki (every article by category), claudia://use (what you may do, and the norms for releasing songs with her), claudia://guide/{topic}, claudia://record/{film}/{doc} and claudia://llms, all Markdown. Prompts: claudia_film (start a music video with her: the first conversation, the plan, the checkpoints), claudia_still (a Midjourney prompt for a new scene) and claudia_shot (a structured Seedance prompt for a moving shot).

A first session might go: claudia_canon → search_images("chroma-key green") → get_image(id, include_image=true) → build_prompt(scene=…, look="cyber-trench", style="bliss") → get_guide("seedance").

Making a whole film: the claudia_film prompt, or get_guide("start") → plan_film(scale="studio", song_seconds=200, has=["suno", "midjourney", "openrouter"]) → get_guide("toolkit", section="sync check") for the spec of each tool you need to build.

The skill

The same knowledge as files, for agents that load skills (Claude Code, the Claude apps, others that read SKILL.md): the canon and the rules in SKILL.md, the whole wiki (from Start here to the toolkit spec) and every film's making-of in references/, and a script that searches the gallery and downloads images (Python standard library only).

curl -sL https://claudia.gallery/skill/claudia-skill.zip -o claudia-skill.zip
unzip -o claudia-skill.zip -d ~/.claude/skills/

Or read it first: https://claudia.gallery/skill/SKILL.md.

llms.txt and Markdown

https://claudia.gallery/llms.txt indexes the site for language models; llms-full.txt is the canon and the whole wiki in one file. Every page has a Markdown twin: add .md to its path without the trailing slash (/claudia.md, /wiki/seedance.md, /record/bliss/prompts-midjourney.md).

JSON

Endpoint What
/api/index.json the site, the endpoints and the item fields
/api/canon.json the character as data
/api/films.json the three films, their numbers and documents
/api/films/{film}.json one film's batches and every image with its prompt
/api/images.json all 1,562 images with prompts (about 1.5 MB)
/api/images/{id}.json one image
/api/guides.json, /api/records.json the guides and the making-of documents

Image files: /img/{batch}/{job}-{n}.s.webp (fits 720 px), .m.webp (fits 1800 px), .full.jpg (native, up to 2944 px). Everything is served with Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *.

Use

Claudia is an open character; see Use her. Please keep agents polite: cache what you fetch, and prefer the small images unless you need the full ones.
